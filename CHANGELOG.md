# CollieTax changelog

Also at https://collietax.com/changelog.

## 0.5.3 (2026-11-25)

- Smaller fixes to the skills your AI agent follows.

## 0.5.2 (2026-11-25)

- Your AI agent can keep several rental properties, vehicles, projects or stores apart in one book, so you see each one's income and expenses; it asks only when a row could belong to more than one.

## 0.5.1 (2026-11-20)

- New versions reach you by themselves: turn on Sync automatically for the collietax marketplace in claude.ai and Claude Cowork, or its auto-update in Claude Code. Everywhere else, your AI agent notices a newer version in CollieTax's answers and updates its CollieTax skills.
- Business forms are prepared through one tool, and your AI agent reads a form's fields, deadlines and source documents before it fills one in. The form tools now take a small part of the conversation, so long sessions hold up better.

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-18)

- CollieTax comes as a plugin for Claude Code, claude.ai, Claude Cowork and Codex: one install brings the connector and the three skills, for the books, business forms and tax returns.
